## Repurposing candidates

CANDIDATE: Oral Minoxidil (low-dose)
CLASS: antihypertensive vasodilator
APPROVED_FOR: Hypertension
WHAT_IT_DOES: Widens blood vessels and, at low oral doses, prolongs the hair-growth phase.
WHY_FOR_THIS_CONDITION: Low-dose oral minoxidil reaches follicles that topical formulations miss.
MECHANISM_TARGET: ATP-sensitive potassium channels in the dermal papilla
REPURPOSE_RATIONALE: Repurposed off-label for androgenetic alopecia when topical therapy is poorly tolerated.
EVIDENCE_STRENGTH: SMALL_RCT
SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE: Retrospective cohorts report meaningful regrowth at 0.25–1.25 mg daily.
REFERENCES: [PMID 33639033](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33639033/)
EFFICACY_HYPOTHESIS: 65% — comparable to topical minoxidil with better adherence
SAFETY: Moderate — hypertrichosis and mild fluid retention are the main issues [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/index.cfm?event=overview.process&drugname=Minoxidil)
CONFIDENCE: Moderate — growing but mostly observational evidence [PMID 33639033](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33639033/)
PATIENT_SPECIFIC_RISKS: Monitor blood pressure and ankle swelling in this patient.
HOW_TO_DISCUSS_WITH_DOCTOR: Ask whether a 1.25 mg starting dose with blood-pressure monitoring is appropriate.

CANDIDATE: Spironolactone
CLASS: aldosterone antagonist
APPROVED_FOR: Hypertension and heart failure
WHAT_IT_DOES: Blocks androgen receptors and reduces androgen production.
WHY_FOR_THIS_CONDITION: Anti-androgen activity can slow androgen-driven follicle miniaturization.
MECHANISM_TARGET: Androgen receptor and aldosterone receptor
REPURPOSE_RATIONALE: Widely used off-label for female pattern hair loss.
EVIDENCE_STRENGTH: OBSERVATIONAL
SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE: Cohort data support stabilization of hair loss in women.
REFERENCES: [PMID 25522096](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25522096/)
EFFICACY_HYPOTHESIS: 50% — stabilizes more than it regrows
SAFETY: Low — hyperkalemia risk; not used in men due to feminizing effects [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/index.cfm?event=overview.process&drugname=Spironolactone)
CONFIDENCE: Low — limited to specific patient groups [PMID 25522096](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25522096/)
PATIENT_SPECIFIC_RISKS: Not appropriate for this male patient given feminizing effects.
HOW_TO_DISCUSS_WITH_DOCTOR: Discuss why this is generally reserved for female patients.
