## 3. Approved Treatments (Backed by Research)

### Drug Card 1 — Finasteride (Propecia)
PROVIDER: Dermatologist or primary-care physician
TREATMENT: Finasteride (Propecia) — 1 mg/day oral
FDA_STATUS: FDA-approved for male androgenetic alopecia
EFFICACY: **80**% — halts hair loss in roughly 80% of men over two years
SAFETY: 90% — generally well tolerated; sexual side effects in a small minority
RISKS: Decreased libido or erectile dysfunction in a small percentage of men.
REFERENCES: [PMID 9777765](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9777765/)

--- ### Drug Card 2 — Dutasteride (Avodart) — Off-Label
WHAT IT DOES: Dutasteride is a pill similar to finasteride that blocks DHT more completely.
PROVIDER: Dermatologist
TREATMENT: Dutasteride (Avodart) — 0.5 mg/day oral (off-label)
FDA_STATUS: Off-label for androgenetic alopecia
EFFICACY: 70% — regrowth in many men in observational use
SAFETY: 80% — side-effect profile similar to finasteride
RISKS: Sexual side effects similar to finasteride; off-label use should be discussed with a doctor.
REFERENCES: [PMID 16635060](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16635060/)
