## Combination ideas

COMBO: Finasteride + Minoxidil
RATIONALE: Pairs an oral DHT blocker with a topical growth stimulator so two independent mechanisms are addressed at once.
EVIDENCETIER: SMALL_RCT
SUPPORTINGEVIDENCE: A 12-month randomized study found combined therapy outperformed either agent used alone on total hair count.
INTERACTIONRISK: Low — no meaningful pharmacokinetic interaction is expected between the two.
PATIENTSPECIFICRISKS: The sexual side effects associated with finasteride still apply when it is combined with minoxidil.
CONFIDENCE: 70% — consistent observational and trial support
HOWTODISCUSSWITHDOCTOR: Ask whether starting both agents at once, or staggering them, is the better approach for you.
REFERENCES: [PMID 17092697](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17092697/)
