## 3. Approved Treatments (Backed by Research)

PROVIDER: Pulmonologist at an ILD center
TREATMENT: Pirfenidone (Esbriet) — 2,403 mg/day oral
FDA_STATUS: FDA-approved for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis
EFFICACY: **45**% — slows the rate of FVC decline by roughly half
SAFETY: 80% — photosensitivity and GI upset are the most common issues
RISKS: Nausea, rash, and elevated liver enzymes; sun protection is advised.
REFERENCES: [PMID 24836310](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24836310/)

PROVIDER: Pulmonologist at an ILD center
TREATMENT: Nintedanib (Ofev) — 150 mg twice daily
FDA_STATUS: FDA-approved for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis
EFFICACY: 50% — slows FVC decline; reduces acute exacerbations
SAFETY: 78% — diarrhea is common and usually manageable
RISKS: Diarrhea, nausea, and an increased bleeding risk; caution with anticoagulants.
REFERENCES: [PMID 24836312](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24836312/)
